## 5. Thread Hook + Outline (Road to SF)

> shipped an ai interactive-fiction game in a week for elevenhacks. story, voices, images, real-news grounding — all generated fresh per game. five engineering things that almost killed it:

**Type:** thread hook (first tweet of thread)
**Characters:** 190
**Project:** roadtosf

**Thread outline:**
1. Hook (above)
2. **Cost bug:** base64 image dataUrls in sessionStorage. partialize stripped them on rehydrate, every refresh re-fired image-gen, $0.30/reload. fixed with vercel blob `put()`.
3. **Latency hiding:** 5s arc gen + 4s image gen. instead of optimizing the model, pre-fire next episode while player walks final scene of current one. loading screen becomes a level.
4. **Grounding:** weekly haiku 4.5 scrape of real sf tech news → jaccard rank vs player tags → top 4 inject into the prompt as "name verbatim". names real, lines model-written.
5. **Autoplay-blocked typewriter:** browser blocked `audio.play()` on fresh prod tabs. fix: gesture-retry listeners + 6s safety-timer fallback to fixed-cadence reveal.
6. **Cast persistence:** snapshot {name, voiceId, look} per character into run state, pass back as constraints to next episode. cap 1 real cameo per episode so they stay rare.
