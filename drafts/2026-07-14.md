## 5. Thread — Clio App Store submission (7 posts)

### Hook (post 1)

> shipped clio to app store submission this weekend

### Post 2

> 1/ bundle id must match the ASC app record exactly
>
> registered `app.clio.narrator` in App Store Connect? your `ios.bundleIdentifier` in app.json must be `app.clio.narrator` too
>
> off by a dot and the upload silently gets rejected as a different app

### Post 3

> 2/ ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption=false
>
> HTTPS + standard crypto only? set this in Info.plist
>
> otherwise every TestFlight build asks you to file a US export compliance form. one line saves you an afternoon

### Post 4

> 3/ AI data disclosure consent screen naming your vendors
>
> App Review guideline 5.1.2(i): if location goes into an AI pipeline, users must consent before you start tracking
>
> clio names ElevenLabs, Firecrawl, Anthropic on first launch

### Post 5

> 4/ TestFlight rendered every custom native view as "Unimplemented component"
>
> cause: bare `ios/` in .gitignore excluded modules/glass-view/ios + modules/native-sheet/ios
> fix: narrow to /ios/ at repo root. add !modules/*/ios

### Post 6
