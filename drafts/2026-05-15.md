## 5. Thread — 4 design rules from shipping roadtosf in 3 weekends

**Format:** thread

### Hook (post 1)

> 4 design rules I shipped roadtosf with. each one inverts a default you'd grab from a hackathon template

### Post 2 — mystery > menu

> rule 1: mystery is the product
>
> never show the player an explicit menu of choices. silent flavor-tag extraction from free text decides which arc fires next. the not-knowing is the reason they keep playing

### Post 3 — latency-hiding via level design

> rule 2: hide LLM latency behind level design
>
> arc gen takes 4-6s with Sonnet 4.6. don't optimize the model. make the loading screen a scene: "you just landed at SFO, your co-founder is texting you"
>
> then pre-fire the next episode while the player walks the current one

### Post 4 — names real, lines model-written

> rule 3: names are real. lines are model-written
>
> Haiku 4.5 scrapes siliconmania.tv weekly into structured JSON. Jaccard(player tags ∩ item tags) ranks. top 4 inject as "name verbatim" constraints
>
> the model never invents quotes. only writes around them

### Post 5 — ship the gotchas as content

> rule 4: ship the gotchas as content
>
> Resend sandbox, Stripe NEXT_PUBLIC at build, base64 dataUrls in sessionStorage
>
> each fix is a post. the bug log is the marketing funnel
