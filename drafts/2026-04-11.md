### Thread outline:
1. Hook (above)
2. The new $100 Pro tier gives 5x Codex usage over Plus, 10x through May 31. That is a direct pricing clone of Anthropic's Claude Max — same number, same "power user" framing, same unlimited Instant/Thinking access. OpenAI didn't pick $100 by accident
3. The usage numbers explain the move: Codex 3M weekly users, up 5x in three months, +70% MoM. Claude Code revenue run rate $2.5B and growing. Both products are now big enough that small pricing changes move millions of dollars per day
4. What used to differentiate these tools was model quality and context length. That gap has collapsed. GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro all land within a few points on real-world coding evals. No one is winning on raw capability for long enough to charge for it
5. So the lever shifts to usage economics. How many agent steps does $100 get you? How often do you hit rate limits mid-task? Can you run parallel subagents without burning the month's credits in a day? These are now the things devs evaluate before switching
6. The loser of a credits war is anyone whose margin depends on metered inference. The winner is whoever built the cheapest serving stack. That is why Anthropic signed 3.5GW of TPU, OpenAI took $40B from SoftBank, and everyone is buying chips. the pricing fight is an infra fight
