## 5. Thread — Clio (5 rules from the voice vibe rebuild)

### Hook (post 1)

> rebuilt clio's voice vibe in July. 5 rules that turned a tour-guide monotone into something closer to a friend who's walked this block before

### Post 2 — rule 1: friend, not guide

> rule 1: friend, not guide
>
> the agent has opinions about what matters here. favorite buildings, boring stretches, things it finds odd. opinion license lets it skip the Wikipedia tone

### Post 3 — rule 2: punctuation as prosody

> rule 2: punctuation as prosody
>
> commas slow it. periods break it. a line without punctuation runs on at pace. silence is a move, not a stall

### Post 4 — rule 3: one clause-initial imperfection, max

> rule 3: one clause-initial imperfection, max
>
> "so, there's this courtyard" works once per segment. twice and the whole thing sounds performed. hedge vocabulary varies with source confidence

### Post 5 — rule 4: in-character bridges before tool calls

> rule 4: in-character bridges before tool calls
>
> "hold on, let me check something" lands cleaner than a 2-second silence while Firecrawl runs. the stitch after an interruption resumes mid-thought

### Post 6 — rule 5: historical present at the climax

> rule 5: historical present at the climax
>
> "he walks out, he sees the river, he decides" beats past tense every time
>
> the past tense is the enemy of standing on the spot where it happened
