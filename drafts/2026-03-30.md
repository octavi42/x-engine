2. The move: Mistral is buying GPUs outright instead of renting from AWS/Azure/GCP. debt financing means they keep full equity while building owned infrastructure.
3. Why it matters: every major AI lab depends on 3 cloud providers for compute. Mistral is breaking that dependency in Europe.
4. The Sweden expansion: second data center planned there. target is 200MW of compute across Europe by end of 2027.
5. For indie builders: Mistral Small 4 (22B params) already outperforms models 3-5x its size under Apache 2.0. if their infra investment makes inference cheaper, open-source models become even more viable for startups.
6. The risk: debt-funded hardware bets are high leverage. if the AI market shifts faster than the hardware depreciates, it could backfire.
7. Bigger picture: the AI race is splitting into US hyperscaler-backed labs (OpenAI/Google/Anthropic) and sovereign infrastructure plays (Mistral in EU, DeepSeek in China). the indie builder benefits from both competing.
