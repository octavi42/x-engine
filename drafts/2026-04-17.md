### Post 2 — the current stack
step 1: describe what you want in natural language. step 2: claude design generates a visual — prototype, deck, or UI mockup. step 3: export the design to claude code. step 4: claude code builds it into a working app. step 5: deploy via vercel, netlify, or your CI.

### Post 3 — what it means for designers
for designers: claude design reads your design system and applies it automatically. consistent output without manual style enforcement. but it optimizes for speed, not craft. the output is 'good enough for a meeting' quality — not portfolio-ready.
