> step 1: yt-dlp pulls the video, ffmpeg extracts audio

> TikToks have music on top of speech. whisper transcribes the music as noise
>
> fix: Demucs first. splits audio into vocals/ and no_vocals/ stems. feed only vocals/ to faster-whisper. transcription becomes readable
