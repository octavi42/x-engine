### Hook (post 1)
an AI voice agent for plumbers just raised $125M at a $1B valuation. 800 customers. on track to book $1B in jobs this year. the most valuable AI startups in 2026 aren't building foundation models — they're answering missed calls:

### Post 2 — the business
avoca handles calls, texts, emails for HVAC, plumbing, automotive, moving companies. not a chatbot — a full AI workforce that books jobs, follows up on leads, and manages the customer lifecycle. 800 businesses using it right now.

### Post 3 — why it works
service businesses lose 30-60% of inbound calls. every missed call is a lost job. avoca picks up every time, qualifies the lead, books the appointment. the ROI math is trivial — one extra booked job per day pays for the whole system.

### Post 4 — the pattern
the biggest AI wins aren't in developer tools or enterprise SaaS. they're in industries with high call volume, thin margins, and zero engineering teams. the plumber doesn't care about your model architecture. they care about the phone ringing and someone answering.

### Post 5 — the takeaway
if you're building an AI startup: look at industries where the "AI" part is invisible. the end user doesn't know or care it's AI. they just know their calls get answered. that's the product. not the model — the outcome.
