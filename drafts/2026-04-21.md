### Hook (post 1)
openai just launched cost-per-click ads inside chatgpt. $3-5/click, $2.4B projected ad revenue this year. the AI assistant-to-ad-platform pipeline is now real. what this means for builders and users:

### Post 2 — the numbers
chatgpt had CPM-based ads since february. now CPC at $3-5/click. advertisers choose views or clicks in the ad manager. openai told investors $2.4B in 2026, $11B in 2027. for context, google did $307B in ad revenue last year. openai wants a piece of that.

### Post 3 — why it matters for AI products
if chatgpt can monetize free users via ads, every AI product will follow. the free tier isn't charity — it's ad inventory. expect claude, gemini, and perplexity to face the same pressure from investors. the subscription-only AI product is now the exception.

### Post 4 — what changes for builders
if you build on the openai API and your users also use chatgpt, your API calls improve their models while their models serve ads to your potential users. the data loop favors the platform. always has.

### Post 5 — the trust question
users got chatgpt for free. now the price is attention. the real question isn't whether AI assistants should have ads — it's whether ads change what the model recommends. when the answer is also the ad placement, trust becomes the product.
