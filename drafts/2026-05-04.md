## 5. Thread hook — Latency-hiding patterns for LLM apps

### Hook (post 1)

> shipped roadtosf in a week: ai interactive-fiction game, story+voices+images all generated fresh per playthrough. the hardest part wasn't quality, it was hiding 10s of LLM gen behind UI that didn't feel broken. four patterns that worked:

### Post 2 — pre-fire next episode

> pre-fire the next episode while the player walks the final scene of the current one. episode N+1 starts gen during episode N's last scene. by the time the player crosses, scene 0 dialogue + image are pre-warmed. no inter-episode loader needed.

### Post 3 — narrator lobby

> episode 0 takes 10s. instead of a spinner: a narrator lobby. free-text q&a powered by haiku 4.5, in-character no-spoilers prompt. player asks anything while gen runs, continue unlocks when episode is ready. loading became gameplay.

### Post 4 — stream arc-gen beat-by-beat

> stream arc-gen beat-by-beat. don't wait for the full arc structure to finish. yield each beat as the LLM produces it; first scene-gen starts mid-arc. cuts dead air from scene-3 dare to scene-4 cold open by ~3s.

### Post 5 — pre-fire image-gen at plan-ready

> pre-fire image-gen at plan-ready, not scene-cross. as soon as next episode plan lands, fire per-scene image-gen POSTs in parallel and cache the URLs. by the time the player crosses, images are done. ~$0.10 wasted if they end the run; worth it.

> try it → roadtosf.com. built solo for elevenlabs hackathon #5.
