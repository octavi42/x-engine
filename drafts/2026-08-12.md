## 5. Thread hook + outline — autonomous-company-tool GDPR DPIA (metadata is still personal data, 5 posts)

**Hook (post 1)**
> metadata-only observation sounds like a GDPR-safe shortcut for AI-agent readiness mapping
> 
> it isn't. under GDPR, metadata that identifies a person IS personal data. calendar attendees, slack sender IDs, doc-edit actor fields, all in scope

**Post 2 — the DPIA baseline**
> the honest baseline for an org-graph tool: DPIA before you touch a single log
> 
> Article 35 GDPR: mandatory for systematic large-scale monitoring of employees. that's exactly what four-signal observation does

**Post 3 — what content-stripping doesn't buy you**
> stripping meeting titles and message bodies doesn't take you out of scope
> 
> what stays: who + when + which handoff + which artifact. that combination is still identifying, still monitoring, still Article 35 territory

**Post 4 — three obligations from day one**
> three obligations you cannot bolt on later:
> 
> purpose limitation. write what "automation candidate ranking" means before you collect
> retention. delete raw graph data on a schedule
> DSAR readiness. an employee can ask what you inferred about them

**Post 5 — the wedge**
> this isn't a legal footnote. it's the wedge
> 
> Celonis-style process mining sells to compliance because compliance already owns the DPIA. the autonomy-planning tool for founders eats that story if you show up with the DPIA on day one, not month six
