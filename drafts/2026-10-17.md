### Post 2 — Resend sandbox sender

> Resend default onboarding@resend.dev only delivers to your account owner email. every visitor got a 403 on OTP. fix: verify domain, set RESEND_FROM, redeploy

### Post 3 — Stripe NEXT_PUBLIC bake time

> NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY bakes at build time on Vercel. set it before the prod build. after swapping live keys: uncheck 'Use existing Build Cache' or you're shipping the wrong key
