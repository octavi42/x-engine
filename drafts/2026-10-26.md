## 5. Thread — Clio place research v5: 4 stages that didn't exist in v4

### Post 2 — type-conditioned question templates

> stage 1: type-conditioned question templates
>
> a church gets different research questions than a brutalist housing block
>
> each place type has a template of 6-8 queries. the LLM picks a template from the Wikidata type, not from the name
>
> cuts the "generic plaque" problem by ~70%

### Post 3 — novelty gating in reflective waves

> stage 2: novelty gating in reflective waves
>
> first wave: the obvious story
> second wave: look at what's already been said, ask only for what's missing
>
> without this, every wave repeats "built in 1876" three times
>
> the gate is a cheap haiku 4.5 call. runs in ~400ms

### Post 4 — trajectory prefetch of scouted POIs

> stage 3: trajectory prefetch
>
> while you walk, we scout POIs ahead on your path and pre-research them
>
> if you pass within 30m they're already cached
>
> cuts GPS-to-audio latency from 3s to under 1s on the second pin onward

### Post 5 — slot-structured PlaceDossier

> stage 4: slot-structured PlaceDossier
>
> not a free-form markdown blob. a struct with slots: founding, people, conflict, ordinariness, pointable_anchors
>
> the narrator reads from slots, not prose. less hallucination, better pacing, UI shows what we know
