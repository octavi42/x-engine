## 5. Thread — Road to SF "mystery is the product" design principle

**Format:** thread (multi-tweet — thread output NYI per format-references.md, drafted as outline)

### Hook (post 1)

> built an ai game with no menus, no settings, no "pick your vibe" dropdown
>
> mystery is the product. 5 posts on why

### Post 2 — the silent extraction

> 1. the silent extraction
>
> the player types one paragraph pitching their startup. that paragraph extracts ~12 flavor tags silently
>
> no menu. no checkbox. the model writes the rest of the run against those tags

### Post 3 — the menu trap

> 2. the menu trap
>
> a "pick your tone" dropdown kills suspense. now the player KNOWS the system has settings, and questions every output against them
>
> mystery dies the moment it's surfaced

### Post 4 — how it compounds

> 3. how it compounds
>
> the player can't tell if a cameo is hardcoded or extracted from their pitch. can't tell if a story beat is canned or model-written
>
> ambiguity is the engagement layer

### Post 5 — the bet

> 4. the bet
>
> roadtosf has 0 explicit personalization knobs. every output is a black-box reaction to the player's first paragraph
>
> the next episode pre-fires before they see this one end
