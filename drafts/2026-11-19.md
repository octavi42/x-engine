## 3. Technical tip — Clio Step inside backend: ETag + rate limits + R2 + 12-month retention

> Clio Step inside backend: a scene catalog that's 99% static, requests that get hammered, privacy policy with a 12-month floor
>
> stack: ETag on the catalog, per-IP rate limit on requests, Cloudflare R2 store, 12-month deletion window
>
> boring is the feature

**Format:** stack-reveal (constraint → stack list → design principle one-liner, single-tweet)
**Type:** technical tip — Clio's Step inside backend has two endpoints and two constraints. `GET /api/step-inside/scenes` returns a slow-moving catalog that the app merges with 8 bundled scenes: cache-friendly, so an ETag on the response lets the app skip the bytes on a 304. `POST /api/step-inside/requests` accepts typed place requests from users: open to spam, so a per-IP rate limit sits in front. Scene bundles are binary panoramas: R2 holds the blobs (local disk in dev). Privacy policy locks requests to a 12-month retention window, after which the typed place is deleted. Four moving parts, zero novel infra. The design principle: boring is the feature when the frontend is already a WebView panorama with offline fallback.

## 5. Thread — UGC Discovery Demucs vocal-isolation before Whisper

### Hook (post 1)

> Whisper mishears every TikTok with music under it. UGC Discovery scores creators off what they actually say, so every garbled transcript is a false reject. fix: run Demucs first. here's the shape

### Post 2 — The problem

> 1/ the problem. TikToks come with a music bed under the voiceover. Whisper trained on clean speech drops words, invents phonemes, sometimes returns one word for a 15s clip. unusable for scoring

### Post 3 — Demucs stems

> 2/ Demucs splits the audio into four stems: vocals, drums, bass, other. open source, PyTorch, runs on Apple Silicon in minutes per clip. use only the vocals stem downstream

### Post 4 — Whisper on vocals-only

> 3/ faster-whisper on the vocals stem. same model, cleaner signal. transcripts recover the dropped words and the phoneme hallucinations disappear. score inputs become reliable

### Post 5 — Unit cost

> 4/ unit cost. Demucs runs local, zero API spend. a few minutes per clip of CPU time on an M-series laptop. the cost of a reliable transcript is a few Watt-hours, not a per-clip API bill

### Post 6 — Payoff

> 5/ payoff. a creator scorer that reads what they said is the whole product. without Demucs the pipeline scores music videos as "low signal" when they just had a loud chorus. fix the ear before you judge the voice
