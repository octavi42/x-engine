## Banned phrases
Never use any of these in drafts:
- "game-changer"
- "dive deep"
- "here's the thing"
- "let me explain"
- "thread :thread:"
- "hot take:"
- "unpopular opinion:"
- "let's talk about"
- "I'm excited to announce"

## Banned punctuation in post bodies
Em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) are AI tells and must not appear in any draft body, refined line, or thread post. They read as machine-generated rhetorical setup-and-reveal.

Replace with: period, comma, colon, or a newline. Examples:
- "stays disabled forever — fix is..." → "stays disabled forever. fix:"
- "background music drowns out — Demucs first" → "background music drowns out. fix: run Demucs first"
- "agents inherit your shell — that's the threat model" → "agents inherit your shell. that's the threat model"

Hyphens (-) are allowed ONLY inside compound modifiers ("launch-day", "self-hosted", "open-source", "build-time"). Never as a rhetorical pause connector.

Arrows (→), colons, and newlines are the preferred connectors.
