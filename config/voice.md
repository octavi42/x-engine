## Banned phrases
Never use any of these in drafts:
- "game-changer"
- "dive deep"
- "here's the thing"
- "let me explain"
- "thread :thread:"
- "hot take:"
- "unpopular opinion:"
- "let's talk about"
- "I'm excited to announce"

## Banned punctuation in post bodies
Em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) are AI tells and must not appear in any draft body, refined line, or thread post. They read as machine-generated rhetorical setup-and-reveal.

Replace with: period, comma, colon, or a newline. Examples:
- "stays disabled forever — fix is..." → "stays disabled forever. fix:"
- "background music drowns out — Demucs first" → "background music drowns out. fix: run Demucs first"
- "agents inherit your shell — that's the threat model" → "agents inherit your shell. that's the threat model"

Hyphens (-) are allowed ONLY inside compound modifiers ("launch-day", "self-hosted", "open-source", "build-time"). Never as a rhetorical pause connector.

Arrows (→), colons, and newlines are the preferred connectors.

## No trailing period at any paragraph end
Drop the period at the end of EVERY paragraph in a post body, plus the end of every refined line and thread post. A paragraph is any line followed by a blank line, plus the last line of the body. Sentence-internal periods inside a paragraph are fine. The trailing period reads formal and AI-typed; X convention is to leave it off.

Multi-paragraph body example:

WRONG (period at end of every paragraph):
```
roadtosf grounds stories in real SF tech news.

Haiku 4.5 scrapes siliconmania.tv/weekly → structured JSON. Jaccard ranks. top 4 go in.

names are real. lines are model-written.
```

RIGHT (drop the trailing period in each paragraph; keep the internal ones):
```
roadtosf grounds stories in real SF tech news

Haiku 4.5 scrapes siliconmania.tv/weekly → structured JSON. Jaccard ranks. top 4 go in

names are real. lines are model-written
```

Notice: "JSON. Jaccard" and "real. lines" keep their internal periods; only the period at the end of each paragraph is dropped.

Single-line example:
- "fix: on mount, re-fire fireBeat for the pending beat index." → "fix: on mount, re-fire fireBeat for the pending beat index"

Question marks (?), exclamation points (!), arrows (→), and code/numbers at paragraph end (e.g. `$0/video`, `Sonnet 4.6`) are unaffected. Colons (:) at paragraph end are also fine.
